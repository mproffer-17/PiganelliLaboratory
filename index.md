<!-- ======================================================
     INTRODUCTION
     ====================================================== -->

<section class="home-intro">

  <div class="home-intro-heading">

    <span class="home-section-kicker">
      Our Science
    </span>

    <h2>
      From immune activation to beta cell dysfunction
    </h2>

  </div>


  <div class="home-intro-content">

    <p>
      Type 1 diabetes develops through a complex interaction between the immune
      system and insulin-producing beta cells. The Piganelli Laboratory studies
      this relationship across multiple biological scales — from molecular
      signaling and cellular metabolism to antigen-specific immunity and
      translational disease models.
    </p>

    <p>
      Our work combines fundamental immunology with approaches designed to
      understand when disease begins, how autoimmune responses evolve, and how
      those responses might ultimately be redirected.
    </p>

  </div>

</section>

<section class="home-philosophy">

  <div class="home-philosophy-copy">

    <span class="home-section-kicker">
      From Mechanism to Translation
    </span>

    <h2>
      Connecting immune biology with disease progression
    </h2>

    <p>
      Our research spans basic and translational immunology. We use complementary
      experimental systems to understand how autoreactive T cells become activated,
      how beta cell stress alters immune recognition, and how measurable immune
      signals may reveal disease before overt diabetes develops.
    </p>

    <a
      class="home-text-link"
      href="{{ site.baseurl }}/research.html"
    >
      Learn more about our research approaches →
    </a>

  </div>


  <div class="home-philosophy-points">


    <div class="home-philosophy-point">

      <span>
        01
      </span>

      <div>

        <strong>
          Mechanism
        </strong>

        <p>
          Define the molecular and cellular pathways that regulate autoimmune
          activation.
        </p>

      </div>

    </div>



    <div class="home-philosophy-point">

      <span>
        02
      </span>

      <div>

        <strong>

        </p>

    <div class="home-philosophy-point">

      <span>
        03
      </span>

      <div>

        <strong>

          human disease.
        </p>

    </div>


  </div>
